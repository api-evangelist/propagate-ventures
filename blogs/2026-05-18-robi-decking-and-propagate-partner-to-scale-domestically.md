Robi Decking, the premier supplier of sustainable outdoor wood products including Black Locust , and Propagate, a leading agroforestry development company, today announced a strategic partnership to develop a reliable, domestically grown supply of high-quality Black Locust (Robinia pseudoacacia) for decking, siding, lumber, and outdoor building products.
