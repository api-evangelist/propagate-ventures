Curly poplar may be the “X Factor” for growing high quality wood in damp soil. This tree tolerates silt, clay, seasonal flooding, and a high water table — and it yields sawlogs sooner than most other tree species in the eastern United States. Curly poplar mitigates pollution and turns it into economic and ecological value on real farmland.
